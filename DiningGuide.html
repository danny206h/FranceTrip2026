Evaluation Criteria

Restaurants are ordered by a unified rank created from the current dining data and your stated preferences.

35% Taste Fit Regional identity, classic or grounded cooking, ingredient quality, and whether the restaurant matches your style.
25% Public Proof Public rating, review volume, consistency, and guide signals such as Michelin or Bib Gourmand.
25% Value Cost relative to quality, lunch formulas, menu flexibility, and whether the meal feels worth the spend.
15% Atmosphere Sense of place, room character, wine-country or Alsatian/Bordeaux feel, and usefulness for the trip.

Rank: the overall audited order across all cities. Lower rank is better.

Rating: the personalized taste-match score from the restaurant card, separate from public reviews.

Review: the strongest public-proof signal found in the card data, such as Tripadvisor, TheFork, Michelin, or Bib Gourmand.

Tags: filters such as Best value, Traditional, Modern, Atmosphere, Lunch, and Fancy Dinner are derived from the restaurant data and meal notes.

Selection philosophy

Your Restaurant Filter

Restaurants are ranked for your priorities: 35% taste fit, 25% public reviews and review volume, 25% value, and 15% atmosphere and sense of place. Moderate-cost restaurants can outrank expensive ones when the food and setting are comparably strong. Public ratings remain separate from the personalized taste-match score, and famous but polarizing choices are visibly marked as iconic exceptions.

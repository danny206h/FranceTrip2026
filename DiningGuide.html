Your Restaurant Filter

The guide favors restaurants with excellent cooking, a distinctive point of view, genuine character, and prices that still feel rational. Regional identity and a strong sense of place matter, but traditional and modern restaurants are judged on equal terms. Creativity is welcome when it makes the meal better; novelty, fusion, or trendiness earns no automatic bonus.

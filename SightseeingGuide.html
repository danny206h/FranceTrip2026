Trip strategy

See the cities at the right hour

This guide is deliberately biased toward walking and neighborhood feel. Bordeaux is best understood as an urban ensemble of stone architecture, riverfront, wine-trade history and elegant everyday neighborhoods. Strasbourg has more obvious monuments, but its real advantage is the contrast between medieval Alsace, imperial Neustadt, leafy residential quarters and university life.

Safety + comfort

Think in terms of comfort, not fear

Both central Bordeaux and Strasbourg are appropriate for ordinary visitor walking. In crowded tourist and transit areas, protect phones and bags from opportunistic theft. For late returns, prefer active, well-lit streets over empty parks or quiet institutional blocks. Bordeaux's Saint-Michel/station side feels more mixed than Jardin Public or the Golden Triangle; Strasbourg's Krutenau feels livelier at night while Orangerie and Neustadt become quieter.
